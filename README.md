# grace-website
This website is my first project, and I hope to learn as I go while branding myself to the world. I hope this will serve as an inspiration to other youths in my community.

It is made up of 4 sections, the about me, activities, education and my contact info:
    The about me section is a short introduction of myself, my ambitions and my goals 
    The activities section is about the events and projects I have carried out. 
    The education section is about the career I project for myself and the online courses and programs I am doing. 
    The contact info are the links to my pages. 
    There is also a gallery that shows all the pictures of the programs I have organised and all those I have participated in
